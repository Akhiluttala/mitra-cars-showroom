BEFORE YOU BUY

A little clarity. A better start.

How do I choose the right car?

Start with your budget, usual passenger count, parking space and driving habits. Use the brand and body-style filters above to create a shortlist, then compare the features that matter most to you.

Are the gallery models available at the showroom?

The gallery is an illustrative model guide, not a live inventory. Call Mitra to confirm local availability, variants, current prices and expected delivery timelines.

What should I ask before booking a car?

Request a detailed on-road quote, confirm the exact variant and included features, and ask about insurance, warranty, booking terms and delivery timing. Discuss any finance or exchange requirements directly with the team.
